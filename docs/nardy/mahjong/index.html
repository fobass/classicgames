Mahjong Garden

Mahjong solitaire with big, clear tiles, and a promise: every deal can be won. No ads, no account, no timers, plays offline. One-time purchase, no subscription.

Support

Questions, a problem, or an idea? Email isabekove@gmail.com. A screenshot of the board helps.

The never-stuck promise

Every deal is built so it can be cleared and checked before you play. Undo, hints and shuffles are free and unlimited. If a move makes the board impossible to clear, the game tells you and offers Rewind to safety, which goes back to the last position that could still be cleared. You can turn the warning off in Settings.

How to play

Take away matching pairs of free tiles until the board is clear. A tile is free when nothing lies on top of it and its left or right side is open. Any flower matches any flower, and any season matches any season.

Progress on your devices

Your Journey level and stats are kept in sync through your own iCloud account. A board in progress stays on the device where you started it.
