The never-stuck promise

Every deal is checked by a solver before you play, so it can be won. Undo and hints are free and unlimited. Hint shows a move that keeps the deal winnable; when it can't find one, or the game can tell a move has made the deal impossible to win, Rewind to safety goes back to the last position known to be winnable. Four-suit Spider is the one exception: those deals are too big to check, so they're dealt as they fall.

How to play

Tap a card and it goes to its best place, or drag it where you want it. Klondike and FreeCell: build four foundations by suit from ace to king, and build the columns down in alternating colours. Spider: build runs from king down to ace in one suit; a full run leaves the table by itself.

Progress on your devices

Your stats and solved daily deals are kept in sync through your own iCloud account. A game in progress stays on the device where you started it.
