Fireside Solitaire Privacy Policy

Fireside Solitaire collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it works offline.

Your games and statistics are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, your statistics are synced through your own iCloud account (Apple’s key-value storage); we never see them. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your achievements and leaderboard scores under Apple’s privacy policy. Purchases are processed by Apple; we receive no personal information.
