Checkers

Russian, English (American checkers), Brazilian and International draughts, each played exactly by its own rules. No ads, no account, plays offline. One-time purchase, no subscription.

Support

Questions, a rule you think is wrong, or a problem? Email isabekove@gmail.com. If it's about a position, a screenshot helps.

Draw rules

A game is drawn by threefold repetition, by agreement, after a run of king moves with no capture (15 moves each in Russian, 20 in Brazilian, 25 in International draughts; 40 moves each without a man move or capture in English draughts), and in small endgames against a lone king (Russian: three kings against one, 15 moves; International and Brazilian: 16 moves with three pieces, 5 with two). The game shows a countdown when a draw rule gets close.

Online games

Online games use Apple's Game Center turn-based matches. To play, sign in to Game Center in the Settings app.
