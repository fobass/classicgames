Checkers Privacy Policy

Checkers collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it works offline.

Your games and statistics are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, your statistics are synced through your own iCloud account (Apple’s key-value storage); we never see them. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your scores, achievements and online games under Apple’s privacy policy: in an online game, the moves and your Game Center nickname are shared with your opponent through Game Center. Purchases are processed by Apple; we receive no personal information.
